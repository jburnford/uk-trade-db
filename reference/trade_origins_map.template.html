What you are looking at

Import quantities and values from the UK Annual Statement of Trade (1872–1899), reconstructed by OCR and reconciled against the Statement's own Tier‑1 national totals. Each commodity is one curated series (the curated whitelist of 1,235; glue‑phantom and duplicate labels have been folded away).

Consignment, not origin

The Statement records the country whence goods were consigned to the UK — the last port of shipment. This is a fact about trade routes, not production. Great entrepôts (Holland, Belgium, the Hanse ports, Gibraltar, Hong Kong, the Straits) re‑shipped goods produced elsewhere and so are systematically overstated as "origins".

Placement at ports of shipment

Each origin sits at its principal port of consignment, not a geographic centroid: United States → New York, Canada → Montreal, Germany → Hamburg, Austria‑Hungary → Trieste, China → Shanghai, Russia → St Petersburg. Colonial umbrellas (British East Indies, Australasia, British Possessions in South Africa) resolve to their constituent colonies where the ledger itemises them. About 90% of located‑origin value is placed; the remainder — residual "Other Countries" lines and un‑gazetteered minor origins — is reported as an unmapped total, never silently dropped.

Provenance tiers

Each origin cell carries a confidence tier from the reconciliation pipeline: A anchored to a national total, B intermediate, C weakly supported. Bubble darkness encodes the tier; bubble area encodes the measure.

Reconciling the origins

A printed origin table often lists a parent total and its breakdown — "United States of America" alongside "on the Atlantic / on the Pacific", or "British East Indies" alongside Bombay, Madras and Bengal. Naïvely summing every line double‑counts, which made raw cotton (and ~50 other staples) read up to 1.8× their true volume in some years and produced a spurious "collapse" wherever the breakdown lines switched on or off. Origins are therefore de‑duplicated to the finest level available, and the national total is taken from the Statement's own Tier‑1 figure, so the two reconcile.

Draft status

This is a first draft for review. Value figures are provisional (value‑side reconciliation is pending; only quantities are anchored). The sugar commodity family and a set of country‑as‑article label defects remain queued. Basemap is a schematic equirectangular graticule, not a boundary map — appropriate to origins that are ports and shifting historical entities.
