The Canadian Supply Network

The probable geography of what Canada consumed by way of Britain: the places that supplied each commodity, whether it was forwarded through Britain unchanged or worked up in a British industry first — named and ranked, not quantified.

By supplier — what each place sent

By commodity — where each came from

Foundation layer, not a measurement. Suppliers are the major consignors in the UK's own import tables for the commodity (forwarded goods) or for its raw material (British-made goods); the material behind each British good is an assumption listed on the companion pages, and the raw materials Britain grew or mined itself are simply "Britain". Rank is shown; value is not. Consignment ports stand in for producers, so Holland and Belgium appear where the Rhine and beyond are meant.

What the lens omits. Anything Canada bought without passing through Britain — across the American border, or Indian and Ceylon tea consigned straight to Montreal — is outside this network by construction.

Data: reports/canada_supply_network_edges.csv (commodity, family, route, British hub, supplier, region, rank) and canada_supply_network.json (with node coordinates) — an edge list ready for Neo4j or Gephi. Script scripts/build_supply_network.py.
