UK → Canada trade explorer

What Canada recorded as imports from the United Kingdom, 1870–1900, by commodity group. Two flows the Statement keeps apart: British produce (exports of UK and Irish manufacture) and re-exports (colonial and foreign goods landed in Britain and shipped on — tea, spices, coffee, rice, silk). Own-year witnesses only; group-name spellings folded; fused-cell repairs applied. Pick up to six. Shading marks years already known to be unreliable — 1871 has no volume, and 1897–1900 lose digits at the page edge.

Reading the chart. A break in a line is a year with no observation, never a zero. The proven % in the tooltip is the share of that year's value sitting inside a printed section whose members sum exactly to the subtotal beside them — the export side has no gold set, so the page proves itself. Low proven does not mean wrong, it means unverified. Coverage varies: a fall can be a fall in what the parser recovered, so check the cell count too.
