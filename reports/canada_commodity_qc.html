What each flag means

  • spike — one year more than 5× its neighbours. The fused-digit signature: two printed columns read as one number.
  • rename twin — another group whose years are almost disjoint from this one and whose name shares ≥40% of its tokens. The signature of a printed label changing, which otherwise reads as one commodity dying and another being born the same year.
  • zero exit / zero entry — present early and gone at the end, or the reverse. Often a rename or a lost group heading rather than trade ending.
  • interior gap — missing years inside the run. 1871 is excluded: no volume covers it.
  • low proven — under 40% of cells sit in a printed section that closes. Not necessarily wrong, but unverified.
